# Literature Review

## Project Title

HealthVerse AI – An Integrated AI-Powered Healthcare Ecosystem

## Introduction

The literature related to HealthVerse AI can be grouped into four
major research areas:

1. Location-Based Healthcare Accessibility
2. Patient Digital Twins
3. Digital Health Records and Retrieval-Augmented Generation
4. Smart Hospital Management

These research areas provide the foundation for the proposed HealthVerse
AI platform.

---

## 1. Location-Based Healthcare

### Paper

Ponthip Limlahapun and Pranee Rushatamukayanunt (2024),
"Integrating Location-Based Services into a Personal Health Hub Mobile
Application."

### Focus

The paper focuses on integrating location-based services into a
personal health mobile application.

### Relevance to HealthVerse AI

This work is related to the Rural Health Location module of HealthVerse
AI.

Our proposed module extends this concept by using the user's location
and selected radius to identify nearby hospitals, pharmacies, and
diagnostic centers and display their distance and available services.

---

## 2. Patient Digital Twin

### Paper

David Drummond and Apolline Gonsard (2024),
"Definitions and Characteristics of Patient Digital Twins Being
Developed for Clinical Use: Scoping Review."

### Focus

The paper studies patient digital twins and their characteristics for
clinical use and personalized healthcare.

### Relevance to HealthVerse AI

This work provides the foundation for the HealthTwin module.

HealthVerse AI proposes to use available medical history, health
records, lifestyle information, and wearable data to create a
personalized digital representation and provide preventive health
insights.

---

## 3. Digital Health Records and RAG

### Paper

Mohammad Alkhalaf et al. (2024),
"Applying Generative AI with Retrieval Augmented Generation to
Summarize and Extract Key Clinical Information from Electronic Health
Records."

### Focus

The paper investigates the use of Generative AI and Retrieval
Augmented Generation for extracting and summarizing clinical
information from electronic health records.

### Relevance to HealthVerse AI

This research supports the Digital Health Passport module.

HealthVerse AI proposes to store lifelong medical records and use
AI-based retrieval and summarization to help users understand
information contained in uploaded medical reports.

---

## 4. Smart Hospital Management

### Paper

Amjed Al-Mousa et al. (2024),
"A Machine Learning-Based Approach for Wait-Time Estimation in
Healthcare Facilities with Multi-Stage Queues."

### Focus

The paper applies machine learning to estimate patient waiting times
in healthcare facilities with multi-stage queues.

### Relevance to HealthVerse AI

This work supports the Smart Hospital Management module.

HealthVerse AI proposes machine-learning-based waiting-time prediction
along with emergency prioritization, doctor workload analysis, and
patient flow optimization.

# Literature Taxonomy

The reviewed research can be organized into four major categories:

| Research Area | Main Technology | HealthVerse AI Module |
|---|---|---|
| Healthcare Accessibility | Location-Based Services | Rural Health Location |
| Personalized Healthcare | Digital Twin | HealthTwin |
| Clinical Information Management | Generative AI + RAG | Digital Health Passport |
| Hospital Operations | Machine Learning | Smart Hospital Management |

---

# Synthesis of Existing Research

The reviewed studies demonstrate that different AI and digital
health technologies can address specific healthcare challenges.

Location-based services can support healthcare accessibility.
Digital twins can support personalized healthcare.
RAG and Generative AI can assist with extracting and summarizing
clinical information.
Machine learning can support hospital waiting-time prediction.

However, these capabilities are generally addressed as separate
research areas.

HealthVerse AI proposes to integrate these capabilities into a single
healthcare ecosystem.

---

# Closest Prior Work

The closest prior work to HealthVerse AI is the 2024 scoping review by
Drummond and Gonsard on patient digital twins.

This work is closely related to the HealthTwin module because it
focuses on patient-specific digital representations for clinical use.

However, HealthVerse AI extends beyond the digital twin concept by
integrating:

- Rural healthcare location services
- Lifelong digital health records
- RAG-based clinical information processing
- Smart hospital management

Therefore, the proposed system aims to connect personalized patient
analytics with healthcare accessibility, medical information
management, and hospital operations.

---

# Research Gap

Existing studies address location-based healthcare, digital health
records, patient digital twins, and hospital waiting-time prediction
as separate areas.

A unified platform integrating these capabilities is less explored.

HealthVerse AI aims to address this gap by combining the four
functional areas into one AI-powered healthcare ecosystem.

---

# Position of HealthVerse AI

HealthVerse AI is positioned as an integrated healthcare decision
support platform rather than a system focused on only one healthcare
function.

The proposed platform combines:

Rural Healthcare Accessibility
        +
Digital Health Passport
        +
HealthTwin
        +
Smart Hospital Management

to provide an integrated healthcare ecosystem.
